# Bank-link completion and interaction — September 21, 2026

## Design and invariants

The change styles the existing physician bank CTA and connects authenticated
Stripe onboarding endpoints to return/refresh actions in Earnings. The bank
panel remains available when the earnings read fails. Returning from Stripe
never means onboarding succeeded; only a successful live account read can
confirm that Stripe has enabled payouts. Expired links renew only while the
original screen and physician session are current.

Asclepius live and sandbox stores retain their existing schema and IDs. The only
bank endpoint writes remain the existing account ID, operational bank status
and audit events. Guarded account assignment remains the authority. Initial
provider creation gains a user/mode idempotency key; Stripe’s finite retention
is not a permanent exactly-once guarantee. Links are minted fresh for the stored
account. Bank responses are not cacheable. Bank details and tax identifiers stay
in Stripe’s hosted form and are not added to local storage or application logs.

No migration, ledger change, upload, source chart, document, contract, blob tree,
object bucket, encryption key or retention setting changes. Team and community
stores and email providers are outside this change. Manual batch approval and
disabled payment execution defaults remain in place.
