# Watch your HTTP access logs for 404s on these paths for seven days after
# flipping the flag off in production. A 404 here is a live consumer that static
# tracing missed: set the flag back to 1 (instant restore), identify the caller
# from the log line, then decide. Check day 1 and day 2, not just day 7 — real
# consumers surface immediately.
#

#                        Hits here during the dark week are the evidence Phase 5
#                        needs to decide whether anyone still uses the dashboard.
