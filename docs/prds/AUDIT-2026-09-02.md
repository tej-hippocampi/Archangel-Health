# Audit: the Sep 1 meeting against the seven PRDs, and the PRDs against the code

Written 2026-09-02, one day after the PRDs were authored and the first three
groups were built. Two passes, both read-only:

1. The meeting transcript against all seven PRDs, asking what the meeting asked
   for that no PRD owns.
2. Each built branch against its own PRD, asking what the PRD requires that the
   code does not do.

Read this before reviewing the PR train. It exists because the PRDs were written
fast, three groups were built and merged inside a day, and the handoff that
closed that session listed three known gaps. There are more than three.

## Source limitation, stated first

The transcript available to this audit is truncated by the harness at 50,000
characters. It ends mid-sentence during the money and metrics discussion. The
prior planning notes assert that the missing tail closed on process rather than
new features, and no re-supplied copy of the tail exists. Everything below
covers the meeting up to that cut. The money and metrics tab content is
unverifiable from source, so group F's treatment of that tab rests on the
planning notes rather than on the transcript.

## Part 1: what the meeting asked for that no PRD owns

Fourteen requirements. They are not scattered evenly. Three clusters account for
the ones that matter.

### Cluster 1: the liability structure

The meeting built a specific chain of reasoning about synthetic augmentation.
Cases may be tweaked as long as they stay clinically valid, and the control that
makes that safe is the physician's attestation: the doctor certifies the case is
clinically valid before labeling, and a false attestation moves liability to
them and voids the pay for that case. Two pieces of that chain are unowned.

**U1. The physician contract and its e-signature, including the pay-depends-on-
quality stipulation.** The meeting put this in the first onboarding step, before
any labeling. `prd-applicant-funnel.md:280` defers it to PR-3, and neither PR-3
PRD accepts it: `prd-payments-rail.md` covers the money rail and explicitly
disclaims tax documents, and `prd-task-pipeline.md` never mentions a contract.
Searching all seven PRDs for "e-sign" returns exactly one hit, the deferral
itself. Today only the seven attestation checkboxes exist: no agreement
document, no versioned terms record, no stored signed artifact.

**U2. The per-case clinical-validity attestation.** No PRD contains the word
attestation outside the health-system authority context. Group D leaves
synthetic augmentation as a research question, which is faithful to the meeting,
but that removes the control while leaving the exposure.

### Cluster 2: money for health systems

**U3. The health-system payout rail.** A mutual deferral.
`prd-health-systems.md:264` sends Stripe and ACH rails to group G.
`prd-payments-rail.md:256` sends health-system payouts back out of scope as "a
different rail decision for a different counterparty size". The meeting treated
it as in scope for the portal, including the constraint that there is no Venmo.
Compounding it, `prd-health-systems.md:37` leaves accrual manual, so nothing in
group C computes what a health system is owed either.

### Cluster 3: the reason the profile work exists

**U6. Profile depth driving routing and pay.** `prd-physician-profile.md:13`
restates the meeting's rationale, that a richer profile powers task routing and
higher pay, and then `:247` defers the mechanism to PR-3. Group D has no
routing-by-profile requirement. The completeness nudges therefore ship without
the thing that motivates a physician to answer them.

### The remaining unowned items

**U4. The three qualifying questions on the /partner interest form** (authority
to license de-identified data to a commercial party, ability to de-identify and
date-shift, and how many patients over how many years in which specialties).
Agreed word for word in the meeting. They currently live in post-signup intake,
not on the form. That is not only a form-design question: `prd-health-systems.md:14`
says every submission is the legal audit trail of an authority attestation,
which only holds if the authority question is asked on the form being archived.
Small.

**U5. Exclusivity enforcement on brokering and export.** The meeting's one
constraint on reselling and recombining was the licensing agreement. Searching
all seven PRDs for "exclusiv" returns nothing. Nothing described anywhere
prevents an exclusively licensed subset from being re-exported. Medium.

**U7. Post-meeting automation for physicians**: the automatic onboarding link
plus one-pager after the intro call, and the calendar hook embedded in the
product. The meeting opened on this. Part of it may deliberately live in the GTM
workspace rather than the product, which should be said out loud rather than
left silent. Medium.

**U8. The /partner link on onboarding pages and in emails.** Small.

**U9. Region-crossed community channels** such as neurology-Africa and
neurology-US. Group E builds specialty channels and country channels as separate
axes; the meeting named the crossed form. Small.

**U10. Archangel persona branding**, the founders' photo as the posting account's
icon. The meeting was specific that this is what makes the posts feel personal
rather than automated. Small.

**U11. The admin analysis layer and the community activity summary.** Two
related asks: a layer that analyzes what is coming in, and on the community tab
specifically, a summary of what is going on. Group F's community tab has no
summary, and F R10 forbids any LLM call from the admin frontend. That decision
is sound and separate: a computed summary is not a chat. Medium.

**U12. The health-system referral flows.** Neither the referrer-enters-an-email
path nor the "I am connected to a health system myself" direct link is a
requirement in any PRD. The first is in fact built, on the branch that relands
PR #103. The second is not, and see C2 below. Medium.

**U13. Referrals as its own admin tab.** Group F folds referrals into Money and
Metrics; the meeting listed five tabs including referrals. Small.

**U14. Weekend webinars.** Group E groups webinars with the conference and
podcast as post-seed. The meeting put the conference and podcast post-seed but
discussed webinars as a near-term community activity. Events with RSVP already
exist, so this is mostly a scheduling decision. Small.

## Part 2: contradictions between a PRD and the meeting

**C1. The referral split.** `prd-physician-profile.md:83` states as settled that
the meeting pinned $25 to the referrer and $50 to the referred physician, the
reverse of what runs in production. The transcript says three things, not one:
"the people who refer to get a free $50", then one line giving the referrer $25,
then the closing exchange settling the signing bonus at "$25 for completing the
case". The first and third match production. Only the middle supports the flip.
This was resolved by founder decision: the production values stand, both rates
stay env-configurable, and the PRD is amended rather than the code. See the
second commit on the reland PR for the applicant gate work.

**C2. The health-system referral column.** The meeting explicitly replaced the
free-text note to founders with a direct link to the /partner interest form and
book-a-call, with Aryaa correcting his own earlier description mid-sentence.
`prd-physician-profile.md:129` preserves the note card and treats the column as
a wordiness problem only. The meeting treated it as a functional redesign.

**C3. Case rooms for large orders.** `prd-task-pipeline.md:155` excludes
send-to-all from room creation because there is no roster to introduce. The
reasoning holds for a pure open queue, but the meeting named large orders
specifically, and the meeting's large-order model is not purely open: eligible
physicians are notified, they claim first-come-first-serve, and at that point
there is a roster. A room could be created on claim rather than on send. Worth
a deliberate decision rather than a silent exclusion.

## Part 3: what the PRDs require that the built code does not do

The handoff that closed the Sep 1 session named three gaps. All three are real
and correctly described. Six more were not mentioned.

### Confirmed by the handoff

1. Group B R9, the profile-completeness nudge, is unwired. Worse than stated:
   the due-list method `list_profiles_needing_nudge` does not exist at all, and
   `stamp_profile_nudge` and `profile_nudge_state` have zero test references.
2. Group B R6, the verified card on community member views, is deferred to the
   community work. Accurate: the member block carries overlapping content but
   never calls the card serializer and links no minted card.
3. Group C requirements 11, 12, 14 and 15 are absent.

### Not mentioned by the handoff

4. **Group A R9 and R10, the applicant nudges, are unwired too**, with the
   identical failure mode. The columns, `stamp_applicant_nudge` and
   `list_applicants_needing_nudge` all exist and nothing calls them, and the two
   email builders were never written. Two PRD sections depend on one wiring job,
   not one.
5. **Group A R5, R11 and R12 have no admin frontend.** The queue-readiness
   badge, the ready filter, the dossier practice-case block and the re-tier
   affordance are backend-complete and tested, and unreachable from the admin UI
   without curl.
6. **Group A R2 has no frontend.** The sign-in-link backend is complete and
   tested; the sign-in door offers no way to request one, so a returning
   applicant who has no password cannot get in.
7. **Group B R7 has no day streak**, which the PRD's D4 required computing from
   `submissions.created_at` at read time.
8. **Group B R3 computes completeness over seven of the PRD's eight factors.**
   Residency or board certification is missing from `_COMPLETENESS_FIELDS`, so
   the percentage has the wrong denominator.
9. **Group A R3 diverges from the PRD as written.** PROVISIONAL grants
   `{TUTORIAL, BROWSE, REFERRAL}`, not `{TUTORIAL, BROWSE}`. This was a
   deliberate and correct fix: narrowing to two silently disabled referral-only
   accounts entirely, because `surfaces()` intersects the access level with the
   account-kind cap and those accounts sit at pending forever. The PRD was never
   amended, so a reviewer reading spec against code hits a false positive.

Minor: three em dashes on added lines, which the repo style rule forbids
anywhere; stale line anchors in `prd-health-systems.md` after a later commit
shifted them; and the health-system referral admin endpoints from PR #103 have
no frontend at all, so three of six funnel stages are unreachable and recorded
rewards are invisible.

### One field no PRD owns and group E consumes

`practice_city` is asserted by `prd-community-activation.md:54` to be added by
the group B profile work. It was not, and it exists nowhere in the codebase.
Group E's city rooms have no data source without it.

## Part 4: deliberate deviations, listed so they can be re-confirmed

These are places a PRD knowingly departs from the meeting and says so. None is a
miss. All are worth a second look before shipping, because each one is a product
decision wearing an implementation detail's clothes.

1. Education is never collected, overriding the meeting's "where have they
   studied", because of the fairness and legal never-collect list. The card
   shows practice facts, not pedigree.
2. One authorized signer binds a health system rather than every member signing.
   Every member is still notified and receives the countersigned PDF.
3. Per-case rooms are group DMs, not channels.
4. Case rooms forbid discussion of case content. Introductions and coordination
   only.
5. The difficulty gate ships measure-only, with the hard requirement as a later
   config flip, because the measured distribution has never been observed with
   live keys and each case costs roughly eight frontier calls.
6. The physician performance panel shows counts and streaks only. Quality
   signals feed the internal score, which stays internal.
7. The practice case is a soft pre-review signal, not a hard block on the
   founders' decision buttons.
8. Pre-approval sessions use magic links rather than a password chosen at
   application.
9. The physician-facing score endpoint is deleted rather than re-gated.
10. No embedded Claude in admin. The meeting landed here too but left the door
    open to a mix; the PRD converts that into a hard no.
11. Admin separation is bundle hygiene, not a second credential store.
12. 1099s are delegated entirely to Stripe. We never generate a form, collect a
    W-9, or see a TIN. The meeting described 1099 generation as partly working;
    delegation is stronger, but it means nothing is generated in house.
13. Automatic calendar invites and Calendly booking webhooks are deferred.
14. First-come-first-serve on health-system data requests is informal: no
    claiming and no reservation.
15. Health-system payout accrual stays manual. This compounds U3.
16. Synthetic augmentation is a research item, not a build. Faithful to the
    meeting, and what leaves U2 unaddressed.

## Judgment

The PRDs are faithful and unusually well grounded on everything they claim. The
pre-approval funnel, the verified card, the health-system broadcast machinery,
the difficulty gate, community activation, admin separation and the Stripe rail
all trace cleanly to the meeting, with deviations argued rather than smuggled.

The gaps cluster. The liability structure (U1 and U2) fell through a handoff
that the receiving PRDs never accepted, and together they are the entire control
the meeting designed around synthetic augmentation. Health-system money (U3) was
lost in a mutual deferral. And the link between profile richness and routing
(U6) motivates work that is being built without it.

On the built side, the pattern is consistent enough to be worth naming: the
backends are complete, tested and correct, and the surfaces that would let a
human reach them are missing. Three separate features (the applicant nudges, the
admin queue affordances, the sign-in link) are each one frontend away from
working.
